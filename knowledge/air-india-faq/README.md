# Air India FAQ knowledge collection

`corpus.json` records the 17 FAQ-index topics plus four adjacent travel-service sources: baggage guidelines, help/contact, flight status, and online check-in information. Each record contains an English topic outline, a short editorial summary, internal source metadata, a review date, and a curated set of paraphrased Q&A summaries. The current local UI has 48 Q&A summaries. It is a useful first knowledge pass, while the source articles contain additional questions that are not yet represented.

The collection intentionally does not reproduce the source pages verbatim. Several pages contain dynamic, route-dependent, medical, payment, loyalty, and historical rules. Their summaries are not sufficient evidence for final chatbot answers. Before RAG uses a topic, extract and validate individual question-answer records, retain source and freshness metadata, and flag items that require current verification. The Vistara integration and Rail & Fly pages need especially careful freshness checks.

The index and source pages were reviewed on 2026-11-04. The user-provided baggage and help paths were not accessible at review time; the current Baggage Guidelines and Contact Us pages were used as their equivalents. The provided web-check-in path was also unavailable; the current `web-checkin` page was used. The FAQ and dedicated web-check-in pages report different cutoffs for online check-in; the corpus flags this rather than merging them. Source URLs live only in this local research corpus for provenance; they are not rendered by the FAQ UI.
